# Product

## Positioning

**Category:** local prospecting and outreach workspace.
**Line:** Find the right local businesses. Start better conversations.

## Users

- Local SEO and Google Business Profile agencies
- Web-design and digital marketing agencies
- Lead-generation consultants and small outbound teams
- Agencies selling AI automation to local businesses

## Core workflow

1. Describe what you sell and who you want to reach (offer profile).
2. Search businesses by niche and location. Parsed filters are shown before any paid search.
3. Enrich results and verify available business email addresses.
4. Inspect why each business matches your offer, with source evidence (Opportunity Brief).
5. Generate and edit personalised drafts.
6. Connect a mailbox and launch a controlled, approved sequence.
7. Manage replies and track qualified conversations.

## Signature feature: the Opportunity Brief

Every researched lead gets a brief with:

- **Observed facts**, each tied to a source record (URL, label, captured excerpt, collection date).
- **Possible opportunities**, labelled as AI suggestions and linked to the facts they rely on.
- **Unknowns**: anything not observable is "Unknown", never proof of a problem.
- **Fit rubric** for the current offer, with visible weights. Unknown criteria do not count. Fit is a prioritisation aid, not a likelihood of buying.
- **Confidence**: how much of the rubric could be checked.
- **Suggested angle** and drafts whose opening line references a specific fact.

The same business can be a strong fit for one offer and weak for another.

## Product principles

- Never fabricate business issues, owners, reviews or verification results. Named people are never guessed.
- Separate observed facts from inferences, everywhere.
- Explicit enrolment: nothing is contacted unless a person adds it to a campaign and approves the exact version.
- Re-check status, approval, quotas, suppression and replies immediately before each send.
- Show estimated usage before work and actual usage after. Failed work is not charged; retries are idempotent.
- Calm, clear, short English. No guaranteed results or inflated AI claims.

## Out of scope until explicitly prioritised

Outlook/IMAP, autonomous AI replies, personalised video, CRM sync, white-label/custom domains, LinkedIn/Instagram outreach, in-house verification, email warmup, agent marketplace.
